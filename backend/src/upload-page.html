Send to Family Vault

Photos and videos go straight to the laptop over Wi-Fi. Anything sent before is skipped.

The first button uses Android's photo picker, which allows at most 100 at a time. For everything at once use Select ALL: open Images or Videos in the side menu, tap ⋮ then Select all. You will see how many are new before anything is sent.

Keep this page open and the screen on while it uploads.
